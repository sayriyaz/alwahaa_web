80th Indian Independence Day: a salute to the Indian community in the UAE

On 15 August 2026, India marks its 80th Independence Day — eight decades since 15 August 1947, when the nation began its journey as a free, sovereign republic. From all of us at Alwahaa Document Clearing, a warm salute to India and to the millions of Indian nationals who call the UAE home.

Freedom. Unity. Progress. A salute to the nation, and a promise for the future.

The Indian community is the largest expatriate group in the UAE — entrepreneurs, professionals, families and workers who have helped build Dubai's economy for generations. Many have trusted Alwahaa with their business setup, visas and government paperwork for over 42 years, and on this occasion we want to thank that community for its trust.

Serving the Indian community in the UAE for over four decades

Whether you are an Indian entrepreneur starting a company in Dubai, a professional sponsoring your family, or a long-time resident renewing your Emirates ID, the paperwork behind each milestone is where Alwahaa steps in. Here is a look at the services Indian nationals in the UAE turn to most.

Business setup & trade licensing

India's entrepreneurial community has long looked to Dubai's mainland and free zones to start and grow businesses. Alwahaa supports:

  • Mainland, free zone and offshore company formation
  • Trade licence issuance, renewal and activity amendments
  • Trade name reservation and MOA amendments
  • Partner changes and establishment card processing

Visa & immigration services

From a first employment visa to sponsoring an entire family, Alwahaa handles the residency journey for Indian nationals in the UAE:

  • Employment, investor, partner and Golden Visa applications
  • Family and dependent visa sponsorship
  • Visa renewals, cancellations and status changes
  • Emirates ID processing and medical typing coordination

PRO & government services

Recurring government work — the kind that keeps a company and a family compliant — is where a dedicated PRO desk saves the most time:

  • MOHRE labour contracts and work permits
  • GDRFA and ICP application support
  • WPS, Tasheel and Amer-related processing
  • Ejari, RTA and RERA-related paperwork

Attestation & legal translation for Indian documents

Indian certificates — educational, marriage, birth or commercial — usually need to be attested before they are recognised in the UAE. Alwahaa coordinates the full chain:

  • Attestation of Indian educational and personal documents
  • MOFA and embassy attestation coordination
  • Certified legal translation (English, Arabic, Hindi and other Indian languages on request)
  • Notary, power of attorney and court-related documentation

See our Indian passport & consular services resource page for the latest on passport renewal, PCC and the current ICAC appointment system.

VAT, corporate tax & compliance

For Indian-owned businesses in the UAE, staying compliant is as important as getting started:

  • VAT registration and return filing
  • Corporate tax registration and assistance
  • Bank account opening support
  • Business and personal insurance coordination

A promise for the future

As India celebrates 80 years of independence, Alwahaa renews its own commitment — to keep serving the Indian community in the UAE with the same trust, precision and excellence that has defined our work since 1983. Happy Independence Day to India, and to every Indian family building their future in the UAE.
